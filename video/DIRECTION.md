# Jeevan Dhara — 60 s demo film: direction

**Format** 1920×1080, 30 fps, 60 s. English narration in a Gnani Timbre voice; the app's real voice exchanges;
a quiet music bed (track to be approved by the user, slot left empty until then). No sound effects.

## Art direction (from Cosmos research, 10 Nov)

References studied on cosmos.so (watched in browser, nothing downloaded):
- *Devanagari poster typography*: Indian scripts at monumental scale, ink on paper, one red; character grids.
- *Swiss editorial grids*: visible hairline grid, big numerals with small labels, a single red field.
- *Exploded-layer product film*: a UI separating into stacked translucent planes to show what it is made of.
- Rejected: dark "data HUD" boards (neon on black, the category cliché).

**World:** cool paper `#F2F2EF`, ink `#121314`, blood red `#D32F2F` (the app's own red), hairline grey `#C9CBC6`.
No gradients, no glow, no glass. Colour does jobs: red = the blood/the answer, ink = facts.

**Type:** one multi-script superfamily, **Anek** (Ek Type) for display in every script (Latin, Devanagari, Kannada,
Tamil, Telugu…), so the languages look like one voice. **Schibsted Grotesk** for small labels and data.
Numbers tabular. Labels sentence case, never tracked caps.

**Signature move:** *the exploded stack.* The phone screen lifts apart into the five layers of the pipeline:
sound → Prisma transcript → structured request (real JSON) → radius rings on the map → Timbre reply.
Secondary motif: concentric rings (the 5/10/15/20 km ladder) used as the only transition.
